## 范文

Dear John,

On hearing the news that you are coming to China to pursue further study after graduating from university, I feel happy from the bottom of my heart. **Here are some suggestions about what to major in postgraduate stage.**

**In the first place**, you'd better decide what career path you want to venture into, which can greatly facilitate sorting out different options. Besides, I am sure if you consider what you are passionate about when thinking about how to choose your majors, you will be confident and motivated to complete your coursework in the future. **Also**, realizing your natural skills and talents can go a long way in helping you make an informed decision when choosing a major, and doing some preliminary tests about the major you are going to select might also be helpful. **These are some important points that I want to mention.**

**Hopefully my suggestions are of benefit to you. I will be delighted to contact you if you require additional information.**

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming
