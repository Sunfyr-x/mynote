## 范文

Dear Professor Smith

On behalf of the student union, I would like to invite you to deliver a lecture on the increasing frequency of extreme weather events.

Recent media coverage about extreme weather events has increase public concern. Many students, including me, are curious about what they signify, or to be more exact, wether they are strong evidence for fatal climate change? If so, have there been or will there be coordinated actions by countries to mitigate its devastating impacts? And how can we college students contribute to these efforts? We would greatly appreciate it if you could share with us your thoughts on these questions and more.

If you would like to accept our invitation, please let us know when you will be available so that we can make further arrangements. We look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
LiMing
