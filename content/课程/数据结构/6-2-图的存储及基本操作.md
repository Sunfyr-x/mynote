## [[数据结构.pdf#page=215&selection=24,0,24,4|邻接表法]]

![[Pasted image 20260827090719.png|300]]
```c
/************************图的临接表存储结构************************/

#define MaxVertexNum 100

typedef struct ArcNode{
	int adjvex;
	struct ArcNode *nextarc;
	//InfoType info;
}ArcNode;

typedef struct VNode{
	VertexType data;
	ArcNode *firstarc;
}VNode, AdjList[MaxVertexNum];

typedef struct{
	AdjList vertices;
	int vexnum, arcnum;
}ALGraph;
```

适合存储稀疏图，访问边表（判断是否存在边）时效率较低
